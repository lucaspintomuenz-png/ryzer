Support

Email pintomuenz@gmail.com and you'll get a reply within two working days. Below are the answers to the questions that come up most.

My alarm didn't ring

Almost always one of three things:

  • Alarm permission. Open RYZER → Settings. The top section says whether iOS is handling your alarms. If it isn't, go to the iPhone's Settings → RYZER and allow alarms.
  • The alarm was switched off. Check the Alarms tab — the toggle has to be on, and the day has to be selected.
  • Your subscription ended. RYZER switches alarms off when a subscription lapses. Settings → RYZER Pro will tell you.

RYZER does not need to be open or running for an alarm to ring. You can close it completely.

The alarm won't stop

That is the point of RYZER — it stops when the mission is done, not before. Complete the push-ups, squats, or photo and it goes quiet immediately.

If you genuinely need out: open RYZER, go to the Alarms tab and switch the alarm off, or turn the phone off entirely.

The reps aren't being counted

  • Prop the phone so your whole upper body is in frame. Leaning it against a wall at floor level works well.
  • Make sure the room has some light — the camera needs to see you.
  • Go through the full range of motion. Half-reps are deliberately not counted.

The alarm is too quiet

RYZER plays through the media volume, not the ringer. Turn the volume up with the side buttons while RYZER is playing a sound, and it will remember that level.

Free trial and billing

How long is the trial?
Three days, free. You are not charged during those three days.
What happens after?
The yearly subscription begins automatically unless you cancel before the trial ends.
How do I cancel?
In RYZER: Settings → RYZER Pro → Manage subscription. Or on your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → RYZER. Cancelling during the trial costs nothing.
What happens when I cancel?
You keep RYZER until the period you've paid for ends. RYZER tells you the day before, and again when it ends. After that your alarms are switched off.
I paid but RYZER is asking me to subscribe.
Tap Restore on the subscription screen. If that doesn't do it, email us.
I want a refund.
Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Deleting your data

Everything RYZER knows is on your phone. Delete the app and it all goes with it. See the Privacy Policy.

Still stuck

Email pintomuenz@gmail.com. Telling us your iPhone model and iOS version makes it much faster to work out what went wrong.
