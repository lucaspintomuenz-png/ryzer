RYZER

Privacy Policy

RYZER collects nothing about you. There is no account, no server, and no tracking. This page explains what that means in detail.

Last updated: 15 September 2026

The short version

RYZER has no internet connection of its own. It does not send your data anywhere, because there is nowhere for it to send data to. Everything the app knows about you stays in RYZER's private storage on your iPhone, and is deleted when you delete the app.

What stays on your device

All of the following is stored only on your iPhone, in the app's own private storage. It is never transmitted:

  • Your alarms and settings — times, days, chosen mission, rep goal, appearance.
  • Your progress — how many mornings you have completed and your current streak.
  • Alarm sounds you record — the audio file, kept until you delete it.
  • Your photo-mission target — if you use the photo mission, the picture you choose as your target is saved so the app can recognise it again.
  • Your answers during setup — used to build your first alarm.

Camera

RYZER uses the camera to count your push-ups and squats, and to check the photo mission. The video is analysed live, frame by frame, entirely on your device. It is never recorded, never saved, and never uploaded. Nothing is kept once the mission ends, apart from the target photo you deliberately chose.

Microphone

The microphone is used only while you are recording your own alarm sound, and only after you start a recording. The resulting file stays on your device.

Motion and alarms

RYZER asks iOS for permission to schedule alarms so they ring reliably even when the app is closed. Those alarms are held by iOS on your device. No alarm information leaves the phone.

Notifications

RYZER schedules notifications locally on your device — as a backup for your alarm, and to tell you if your subscription is about to end. There is no push server involved, so no notification can be sent to you from outside your own phone.

Purchases

RYZER Pro is sold through Apple. When you subscribe, Apple handles the entire transaction. RYZER never sees your name, payment details, or Apple Account. The app only receives a signed confirmation from Apple that a valid subscription exists, which it checks on your device.

Apple's own handling of that transaction is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy. As a developer, Apple provides us with aggregate sales figures — totals and trends that cannot be linked to any individual.

Analytics and tracking

RYZER contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixels, and no third-party software development kits of any kind. Nothing about how you use the app is measured or reported.

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the App Store privacy information will be updated with it.

Sharing and selling

We do not share, sell, rent, or trade your information — a promise that is easy to keep, because we never receive any.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, export, or delete personal data that a company holds about you. We hold none, so there is nothing for us to give you or delete.

The data on your own device is entirely under your control. To remove all of it, delete the RYZER app from your iPhone. To cancel a subscription, open RYZER, go to Settings → RYZER Pro → Manage subscription, or use Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on your iPhone.

Children

RYZER is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it. Material changes will be described in the app's release notes.
