RYZER

Privacy Policy

RYZER has no accounts and never learns who you are. It sends a small set of anonymous usage statistics so we can see what works. Your camera, microphone and photos never leave your phone.

Last updated: 20 September 2026

The short version

  • Stays on your phone: your alarms, your progress, your recordings, your photo target, and everything the camera sees.
  • Leaves your phone: anonymous statistics, such as "a mission was completed in 94 seconds", sent to our analytics provider in the EU. They are never linked to your name, email or Apple Account.
  • Never sent: your name, email, Apple Account, IP address, screen, camera, microphone, or photos.

What stays on your device

All of the following is stored only on your iPhone, in the app's own private storage. It is never transmitted:

  • Your alarms and settings: times, days, chosen mission, rep goal, appearance.
  • Your progress: the mornings you have completed and your streak history.
  • Alarm sounds you record: the audio file, kept until you delete it.
  • Your photo-mission target: if you use the photo mission, the picture you choose as your target is saved so the app can recognise it again.

Deleting RYZER deletes all of it.

Camera

RYZER uses the camera to count your push-ups and squats, and to check the photo mission. The video is analysed live, frame by frame, entirely on your device. It is never recorded, never saved, and never uploaded. Nothing is kept once the mission ends, apart from the target photo you deliberately chose.

Microphone

The microphone is used only while you are recording your own alarm sound, and only after you start a recording. The resulting file stays on your device.

Alarms and notifications

RYZER asks iOS for permission to schedule alarms so they ring reliably even when the app is closed. Alarms and notifications are scheduled locally on your device. There is no push server, so nothing can be sent to your phone from outside it.

Anonymous usage statistics

To understand which parts of RYZER work and where people get stuck, the app sends short, anonymous event reports to PostHog, an analytics service that processes them on our behalf. The data is stored on PostHog's servers in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany). PostHog, Inc. is based in the United States.

What is sent

  • Setup: which setup screens were shown, the answers to the multiple-choice questions, and the mission, rep goal, number of days and wake-up hour you chose.
  • Subscription: when the subscription screen was shown, and whether a purchase or restore was started, completed, cancelled or failed, with the price and currency.
  • Missions: when a mission starts and finishes, which mission, the rep goal, how long it took, and your current streak.
  • App use: when the app is installed, opened, or sent to the background.
  • Technical details: device model, iOS version, app version, language, and an approximate location (country and city) worked out from your IP address.

The app generates a random identifier on your phone so that the events from one phone can be counted together. It is not connected to your name, email, Apple Account, or advertising identifier, and it is deleted when you delete the app.

What is never sent

  • Your name, email address, Apple Account, or any contact details.
  • Your IP address. It is used briefly to estimate a country and city, then discarded and not stored.
  • Anything from the camera or microphone, your photos, or your recordings.
  • Recordings of your screen. Session recording is permanently disabled.
  • Your advertising identifier. RYZER does not use it and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Why

We process these statistics on the basis of our legitimate interest in understanding and improving the app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The statistics are limited to what is needed for that purpose and cannot be linked to you. You have the right to object at any time by emailing pintomuenz@gmail.com.

How long it is kept

Statistics are kept for as long as they help us understand how RYZER is used. Because they cannot be linked to you, we are unable to find, hand over, or delete an individual's events on request. Deleting the app removes the random identifier from your phone; if you reinstall, the app starts over with a new one that is not connected to the old.

Purchases

RYZER Pro is sold through Apple. When you subscribe, Apple handles the entire transaction. RYZER never sees your name, payment details, or Apple Account. The app only receives a signed confirmation from Apple that a valid subscription exists, which it checks on your device.

Apple's own handling of that transaction is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy. Apple also provides us with aggregate sales figures that cannot be linked to any individual.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information. The anonymous statistics described above are processed only by PostHog, on our behalf. There is no advertising in RYZER and no data is shared with advertisers.

We would disclose information only where the law requires it, for example to comply with a binding order from a court or public authority. Because the statistics cannot be linked to you, they could not identify you even then.

Access from outside the EU

The statistics are stored in the EU, but PostHog, Inc. is a company based in the United States, and its staff may access them there, for example to operate or support the service. That access is covered by our data processing agreement with PostHog, which includes the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Art. 46 GDPR).

Security

The statistics travel to PostHog over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Everything else stays in RYZER's own storage on your iPhone, which iOS keeps separate from other apps and encrypts whenever your phone is locked with a passcode.

If RYZER changes hands

RYZER is currently run by the individual named in the legal notice. If the app is later transferred to a company or a new owner, the anonymous statistics described above may be transferred with it, and the new owner will be bound by this policy. We will announce such a change on this page and in the app's release notes before it takes effect.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, export, restrict, object to, or delete personal data held about you, and to complain to a data protection authority. The data on your own device is entirely under your control: deleting RYZER removes it. The usage statistics are anonymous; to object to them, email us at the address below.

To cancel a subscription, open RYZER and go to Settings → RYZER Pro → Manage subscription, or use Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on your iPhone.

Children

RYZER is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it. Material changes will be described in the app's release notes.
