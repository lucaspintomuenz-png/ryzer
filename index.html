RYZER

The alarm you have to earn.

RYZER doesn't have a snooze button. It rings until you've done the push-ups — counted by the camera, on your phone, with nothing recorded. Then it stops, and you're already up.

How it works

  • Set a time and pick a mission: push-ups, squats, or photograph something across the room.
  • The alarm rings full screen, through silent mode, and keeps ringing.
  • It goes quiet the moment the mission is done. Not before.

Privacy

RYZER has no accounts and never learns who you are. The camera counts your reps live on the device and records nothing. The only thing that leaves your phone is anonymous usage statistics that are never linked to you. Read the full privacy policy.

Help
